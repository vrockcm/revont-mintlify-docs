**Communications Hub** is the shared activity surface for meetings and email.

## Choose A Tab

Use the segmented tab control in the page header:

- **Meetings** for the meeting queue, calendar, upcoming activity, and meeting preparation.
- **Emails** for the email list and the selected thread detail.

The sidebar child item is **Meetings & Calendar**. After the hub opens, the header uses the shorter **Meetings** label. The page can automatically switch to the readable tab when a direct link targets a tab the current user cannot access.

## Search And Filter

The header supports **Search...**, client and deal filters, and a sharing filter with options such as **Sharing: Any**, **Shared By Me**, and **Shared With Me**. Depending on your permissions, you may also see **Filter by team members**.

## Meetings

Meeting cards can expose **Join**, **Prep**, and rescheduling controls. The page also provides **Schedule Meeting** and **Start Meeting** flows. Select a meeting to open its detail route.

## Emails

The **Emails** tab uses a master-detail layout. Select a thread to open its detail, then use the actions available for that thread, including compose, reply, linking, sharing, and attachment actions when enabled.

## Sync

Use the page sync control when you need a manual refresh. Email and calendar incremental sync can also run when their tab becomes active. The header displays the current sync state and surfaces errors when a sync cannot start.
