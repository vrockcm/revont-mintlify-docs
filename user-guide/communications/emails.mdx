Open **Communications Hub** from the sidebar, then select **Emails** in the header.

## Find An Email Thread

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Communications Hub">
    Select **Communications Hub** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Emails">
    Select **Emails** in the header.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search or filter">
    Use **Search...**, the client or deal filters, the sharing filter, or the activity filters to narrow the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the thread">
    Select an email thread in the left panel. Its messages and details open in the right panel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Work With A Thread

Review the messages in the thread, then use the available thread actions. Open related client or deal records when you need more context.

You can also use the thread Chat area to ask about the conversation, draft a response, or identify follow-up work.

<Note>
  Email actions and available message history depend on the connected mailbox and the access granted to Revont.
</Note>
