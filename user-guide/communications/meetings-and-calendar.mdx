---
title: "Meetings And Calendar"
description: "Find meetings, review activity, and manage your calendar from Communications."
icon: "calendar-days"
---

Open **Communications Hub** from the sidebar, then select **Meetings & Calendar** to open the hub. In the page header, select **Meetings**.

## Find A Meeting

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Communications Hub">
    Select **Communications Hub** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Meetings tab">
    If the hub opens on another tab, select **Meetings** in the header.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Narrow the list">
    Use **Search...**, the client or deal filters, the sharing filter, or the **All** and **Unlinked** activity filters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the meeting">
    Select a meeting to open its details, transcript, notes, related records, and available Chat context.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Schedule Or Start A Meeting

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the meeting action">
    From **Communications Hub > Meetings & Calendar**, select the page action for **Schedule Meeting** or **Start Meeting**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the meeting details">
    Complete the details requested by the dialog.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save or start">
    Complete the dialog action to save the meeting or start the meeting.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Meeting Follow-Up

Open a meeting and use its Chat bar. Select **Actions**, then choose **Write follow-up email** or **Schedule follow-up** when those actions are available.

<Note>
  Calendar actions depend on your connected account and workspace permissions.
</Note>
