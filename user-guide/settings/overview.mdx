Open **Settings** from the sidebar.

The settings panel is organized into separate tabs. Use the tab that matches the part of Revont you want to change.

## Settings Tabs

- **General** controls startup, meeting notifications, and updates.
- **Voice** controls the microphone and recording input.
- **Integrations** connects Google, Microsoft, and Zoom.
- **Keybinds** changes keyboard shortcuts.
- **Data & Storage** manages backups and local storage.
- **Profile** changes your name, picture, password, and account.
- **Privacy** prepares a download of your data.
- **Billing** shows subscription information.
- **Support** contains help, system information, and diagnostics.
- **Data Model** manages objects, fields, and relations.
- **Quotas** manages quota defaults and targets.

Select a tab in the settings sidebar to open its controls.
