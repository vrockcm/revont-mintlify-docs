title: "Profile Settings"
description: "Update your name, profile picture, password, or account."
icon: "user"
---

Open **Settings > Profile**.

## Update Your Name

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Profile">
    Select **Settings** in the sidebar, then select **Profile**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit Display Name">
    Update **Display Name**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the name">
    Select **Save Name**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Update Your Profile Picture

In **Settings > Profile**, select **Upload profile picture**, choose an image, and select **Save Avatar**.

## Reset Your Password

Under **Password**, select **Send Password Reset Email** and follow the instructions in your email.

## Delete Your Account

The account deletion area requires you to type **DELETE** before the deletion can continue. Review the warning carefully before confirming.
