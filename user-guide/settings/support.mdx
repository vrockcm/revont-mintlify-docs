Open **Settings > Support**.

## Find Help

Review **Help & Documentation** and **Additional Resources** for support links.

## Check System Information

The support page shows **System Information**, including **App Version** and **Third-Party Notices**.

## Share Diagnostics

Use the diagnostics controls shown on the page when support asks you to provide troubleshooting information.
