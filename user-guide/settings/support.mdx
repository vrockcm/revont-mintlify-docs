---

Open **Settings > Support**.

## Find Help

Review **Help & Documentation** and **Additional Resources** for support links.

## Check System Information

Under **System Information**, review **App Version**. Select **View Notices** to open **Third-Party Notices** and review the licenses included with Revont.

## Share Diagnostics

Select **View Diagnostics** to open the **Diagnostics** dialog. Select **Copy** when support asks you to share the displayed system information, or select **Close** to return to Settings.
