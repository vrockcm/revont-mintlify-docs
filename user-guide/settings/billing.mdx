---
title: "Billing Settings"
description: "Review your current plan and manage your subscription."
icon: "credit-card"
---

Open **Settings > Billing**.

Review **Subscription**, **Current Plan**, and **Available Plans**. Organization admins choose monthly or yearly billing, select the initial seat quantity during Checkout, and select **Manage** or **Manage Subscription** to open the Stripe Billing Portal later.

If billing is managed by your organization, the page may show **Contact Your Admin** instead.

Regular members cannot start a subscription, buy seats, change the billing interval, or configure Auto-Recharge. Manage seats and pooled usage from **Organization Management**.
