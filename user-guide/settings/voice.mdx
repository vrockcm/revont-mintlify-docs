---
title: "Voice Settings"
description: "Choose the microphone Revont uses for recording."
icon: "mic"
---

Open **Settings > Voice**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Select **Settings** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Voice">
    Select **Voice** in the settings sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the microphone">
    Under **Microphone**, choose the recording device you want to use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the input level">
    Speak into the microphone and review the **Input Level** indicator.
  </Step>
</Steps>
