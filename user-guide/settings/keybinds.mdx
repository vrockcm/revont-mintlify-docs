Open **Settings > Keybinds**.

## Change A Shortcut

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Select **Settings** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Keybinds">
    Select **Keybinds** in the settings sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the shortcut">
    Select the shortcut you want to change under its group.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press the new keys">
    Press the key combination you want to use, then finish the shortcut edit.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Shortcuts are grouped under **Recording Controls**, **Overlay Controls**, **Application Controls**, and **Navigation Shortcuts**. Overlay shortcuts include **Toggle Chat**.
