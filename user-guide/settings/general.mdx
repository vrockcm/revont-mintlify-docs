---

Open **Settings > General**.

## Change General Preferences

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Select **Settings** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose General">
    Select **General** in the settings sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change startup behavior">
    Under **Startup**, turn **Open at Login** on or off.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change meeting notifications">
    Under **Meeting Notifications**, use **Enable Meeting Notifications**, **Show Meeting Reminders**, **Reminder Time**, **Show "Meeting Started" Notification**, and **Show Meeting Detection Notifications** as needed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check For Updates

Under **Application Version**, review the version and select **Check for Updates**. If an update is ready, select **Restart and Update**.
