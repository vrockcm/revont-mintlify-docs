---

Open **Settings > General**.

## Change General Preferences

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Select **Settings** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose General">
    Select **General** in the settings sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change a preference">
    Update the startup or meeting notification setting you want to change.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check For Updates

In **Settings > General**, review the application version and select **Check for Updates**. If an update is ready, use **Restart and Update** when it appears.
