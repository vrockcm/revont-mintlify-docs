Open **Organization Management** from the sidebar.

## Invite A Teammate

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Organization Management">
    Under **Administration**, select **Organization Management**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Invite Member">
    Select **Invite Member** in the page header.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the invitation">
    In the **Invite Team Member** dialog, enter the teammate's email and choose a **Role**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invitation">
    Select **Send Invite**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The available roles include **Admin** and **Sales Rep**. Pending invitations appear in the **Pending Invitations** section. Invitations do not carry a custom plan or Stripe price. Seat availability comes from the organization's subscription.

## Find A User

Use **Search by name or email** and the role filter. The role options are **All Roles**, **Admin**, and **Sales Rep**.

## Manage An Existing User

Open the row action menu for a member to review the role and account actions available to you. Pending invitations can be revoked from their row.

<Note>
  Organization management is administrator-only. Seat limits and workspace policy can affect invitations. If an invitation would exceed the paid seat quantity, buy additional seats before inviting the teammate.
</Note>
