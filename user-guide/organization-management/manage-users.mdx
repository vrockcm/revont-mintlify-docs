Open **Organization Management** from the sidebar.

## Invite A Teammate

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Organization Management">
    Under **Administration**, select **Organization Management**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the user area">
    Select the user or team management area shown in the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite the user">
    Choose the invite action and enter the teammate's email and access details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invitation">
    Review the details and send the invitation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Manage Access

Open a user from the organization list to review the access controls available to you. Changes depend on your administrator permissions and workspace policy.
