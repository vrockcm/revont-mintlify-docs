Open **Audit** from the sidebar.

## Choose An Audit View

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Audit">
    Under **Administration**, select **Audit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the activity type">
    Select **Activity Log**, **Email Access**, **Meeting Access**, **AI Use**, or **Login History**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter the results">
    Use the filters shown for that view. Activity and login views include search, and access views include actor and target filters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the event">
    Select an event row to open its detail panel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For date filtering, use the date range controls. Select **Clear** when you want to remove the active filters.

## Review AI use

The **AI Use** tab shows organization-wide AI accounting metadata for each operation. You can filter by team member, operation, product area, billing class, outcome, date, or operation ID, then export the filtered metadata when you have export permission.

The view explains what happened and how it was accounted for, including credits, weighted call usage, provider/model identifiers, outcome, and correlation ID. It does not show prompts, full responses, transcript text, recordings, or document contents.

<Note>
  Audit access is permission-controlled. The tabs and records shown depend on your organization access.
</Note>
