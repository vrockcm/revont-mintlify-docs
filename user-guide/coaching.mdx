Coaching has different views based on the current viewer and selected rep.

- Admins can start from an organization view and open team insights or a rep drill-down.
- Reps see their own coaching and growth surface.
- A rep with fewer than two analyzed meetings sees the onboarding empty state until enough analyzed activity exists.

## What the page can show

The current coaching surface is composed of skill mastery, growth signals, meeting feedback, learning path, goals, and rep memory panels. The exact panels shown depend on the viewer state.

Select a team member or a coaching item when the page offers a drill-down. Use the breadcrumb to return to the broader Coaching view.

<Note>
  Coaching is feature-gated. Empty states are meaningful: they may indicate that the workspace has not accumulated enough analyzed meetings yet.
</Note>
