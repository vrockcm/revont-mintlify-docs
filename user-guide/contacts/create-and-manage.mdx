---
title: "Create And Manage Contacts"
description: "Create contacts, search your contact table, and restore deleted records."
icon: "contact"
---

Open **Contacts** from the sidebar.

## Create A Contact

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Contacts">
    Under **Core Modules**, select **Contacts**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a contact">
    Select **New Contact**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the details">
    Complete the fields shown in the contact form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the contact">
    Finish the form to add the contact to the workspace.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Find A Contact

Use the search box and filters above the contacts table. Select a row to open the contact details and related activity.

## Restore A Deleted Contact

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the deleted view">
    In **Contacts**, switch to the deleted records view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the contact">
    Choose the contact you want to restore.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Restore the record">
    Select **Restore Contact**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
