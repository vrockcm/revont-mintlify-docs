Contacts is the organization-wide directory for people connected to clients and deals.

## Find a contact

Use search, client and deal selectors, buyer-role filters, column filters, sorting, and pagination to narrow the directory. Selecting a row opens the contact detail page.

The table can show client, owner, creator, source, last activity, custom fields, and deal role when a deal filter is active.

## Create and manage

Users with contact write access can create a contact from the page action. A new contact can be associated with a client and, when applicable, a deal and relationship.

Row actions support editing and deleting. Deleted contacts appear in a separate Deleted view and can be restored by an eligible administrator. Linked activity history is preserved through the delete and restore flow.
