---
title: "Create And Manage Clients"
description: "Create client records, find them quickly, and restore deleted clients."
icon: "building-2"
---

Open **Clients** from the sidebar.

## Create A Client

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Clients">
    Under **Core Modules**, select **Clients**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a client record">
    Select **Create Client**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the details">
    Complete the fields shown in the client form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the client">
    Finish the form to create the client record.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Find A Client

Use the search box and available filters at the top of **Clients**. Use the list or card view control to change how records are displayed. Select a client to open its details.

## Restore A Deleted Client

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the deleted view">
    In **Clients**, change the record view to the deleted records view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a client">
    Select the client you want to bring back.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Restore the record">
    Select **Restore**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The fields and actions available on a client depend on your workspace data model and permissions.
</Note>
