CRM Integrations is an admin surface for the two-way CRM sync.

## Tabs

- **Connections** connects or disconnects Salesforce or HubSpot and starts a sync.
- **Records** browses and selects CRM records to sync.
- **Field Mapping** edits per-field Revont-to-CRM mappings.
- **Owner Mapping** maps Revont users to CRM owners.
- **Stage Mapping** maps pipeline and stage values.
- **Review Queue** reviews and resolves deduplication candidates.
- **Activity Log** shows sync activity and conflicts.

The page requires the CRM Integrations feature and a read permission. Write controls have their own access checks.

<Note>
  Connect only one active CRM provider when the app indicates that mapping is provider-specific. Review mappings and deduplication candidates before applying changes.
</Note>
