Open **CRM Integrations** from the sidebar.

## Connect A CRM

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open CRM Integrations">
    Under **Administration**, select **CRM Integrations**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the connections area">
    Select the connections area and choose the CRM provider you want to connect.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize the provider">
    Follow the provider sign-in and authorization steps.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the connection">
    Return to Revont and confirm the connection status before starting any sync or mapping work.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Review Mapping

Use the records, field mapping, owner mapping, and stage mapping areas to review how the CRM lines up with Revont. Review any conflicts or duplicate records before confirming a change.

<Note>
  CRM integration changes can affect synced records. Make changes only when you have the required administrator access.
</Note>
