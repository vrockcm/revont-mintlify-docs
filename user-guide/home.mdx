Home is the default protected route after sign-in. It is the orientation surface for the workspace, not a replacement for the task-specific pages in the sidebar.

The current Home experience brings together:

- **Live Assistance**
- **Unified Memory**
- **Connected Pipeline**
- **Universal Search**
- **Meeting Intelligence**

Use the sidebar when you are ready to work in a specific area. Home links and calls to action can also move you into the product and contact surfaces.
