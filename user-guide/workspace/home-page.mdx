Open **Home** from the sidebar.

Home brings together the work Revont has identified for you, including meetings, follow-through, pipeline activity, and other workspace signals.

## Open Home

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sidebar">
    If the sidebar is collapsed, select **Expand Sidebar**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Home">
    Select **Home** at the top of the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open an item">
    Select a meeting, deal, action item, or other card to open its details.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Use Home With Chat

Select a relevant item, then use its Chat area when you want to ask about that item. Chat uses the record or meeting you opened as context.

<Note>
  The cards and information shown on Home depend on your records, connected accounts, and workspace permissions.
</Note>
