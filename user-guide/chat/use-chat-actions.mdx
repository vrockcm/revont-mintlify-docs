Chat can help you turn a conversation into work. Available actions depend on your context and permissions.

## Actions Chat Can Prepare

Depending on your access, Chat can:

- Create clients and contacts after you provide the details.
- Create, update, or remove action items.
- Draft follow-up emails and open them in an editable composer.
- Schedule or reschedule meetings for your review.
- Save, update, dismiss, or delete memory about clients, deals, meetings, or conversations.
- Create, update, delete, or publish Knowledge Base articles.
- Create or update products and competitor records, or create product suites.
- Create, update, or archive custom records.
- Open Data Transfer for an import or export workflow.
- Propose Data Model changes when you are an administrator.

Chat can search and analyze deals, pipeline, forecasts, and dashboards. Creating or editing a deal is currently done from the **Deals** page.

## Draft A Follow-Up Email

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the right context">
    Open the meeting, email thread, client, deal, or contact related to the follow-up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Actions">
    In the Chat bar, select **Actions**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Write follow-up email">
    Select **Write follow-up email**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the draft">
    Review the recipients, subject, and message. Edit the draft before sending.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Schedule Follow-Up

Open **Actions** in the relevant Chat bar and select **Schedule follow-up**. Review the proposed meeting details before saving or sending anything.

## Ask Chat To Make A Change

You can ask Chat to create or update follow-up work, draft an email, schedule a meeting, save useful information, or update a record when those actions are available.

Chat shows what it plans to do. Review the proposed change and confirm it before the change is made.

<Note>
  Chat does not silently send messages or make important changes. Review the confirmation shown in Chat first.
</Note>
