Open **Coaching** from the sidebar.

## Review Your Coaching

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Coaching">
    Under **Insights**, select **Coaching**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a view">
    Review the coaching dashboard and choose the meeting, trend, skill, or learning view you want to inspect.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the details">
    Select a meeting or insight to open its detailed feedback.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the feedback">
    Review the recommendation and use the linked meeting or record to apply it to your next follow-up.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Team and administrative coaching views may only be visible to users with the required permissions.
</Note>
