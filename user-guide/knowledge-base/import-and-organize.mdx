Open **Knowledge Base** from the sidebar.

## Organize Folders

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the folder tree">
    Under **Administration**, select **Knowledge Base**, then review the folders in the left panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a group">
    Select **New Group**, enter the group name, and save it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move a folder">
    Drag a folder to its new location in the folder hierarchy.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Review An Import

When you import content, Revont opens the import review flow. Review the proposed articles and their destination folders in the import stepper before completing the import.

<Note>
  Imported content is not ready for your team until you complete the review flow and confirm the import.
</Note>
