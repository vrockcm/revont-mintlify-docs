# Simple pricing built for sales teams

Every Revont plan includes real-time AI call assistance, transcription, post-call analytics, and AI-powered productivity tools.

Usage allowances are pooled across your organization, giving your team flexibility to use Revont where they need it most. Organization admins purchase seats, and the organization controls billing.

## Monthly and yearly billing

Organization admins can choose the billing interval for the organization's Stripe subscription. The usage model is the same on both intervals. Annual billing changes payment cadence, not the monthly credit grant or the daily call-hour guardrail.

| Plan | Monthly | Yearly |
| --- | --: | --: |
| Revont | \$99 per seat / month | \$990 per seat / year |
| Revont Pro | \$149 per seat / month | \$1,490 per seat / year |

## Organization-wide usage

Included Assisted Call Hours, Unassisted Call Hours, and Revont Credits are pooled across your organization.

For example, an organization with **10 Revont seats** receives:

| Allowance | Organization Total |
| --- | --: |
| Revont Assisted Call Hours | 30 hours / day |
| Revont Credits | 6,000 credits / month |

Individual users are not restricted to exactly one seat's contribution.

If one rep uses more and another uses less, they draw from the same organization-wide allowance.

This keeps your sales team working without requiring admins to constantly redistribute allowances between users.

<Info>
  Revont still tracks usage for every individual user so organization admins can see exactly where Call Hours and Revont Credits are being consumed.
</Info>

## What happens when you reach your allowance?

Call usage uses a pooled weighted allowance. In the initial rollout, Revont blocks a new call when the organization's allowance is exhausted. There is no automatic call overage charge. Assisted and unassisted calls use the same predictable meter, with unassisted calls weighted lower because live suggestions are disabled.

Revont Credits use **Auto-Recharge** to keep your team's AI tools available without interruption.

Organization admins configure the recharge package and usage threshold.
