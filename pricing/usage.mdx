# Usage & Auto-Recharge

Revont is designed so sales reps don't lose access to AI tools in the middle of their workday.

Call Hours and Revont Credits are managed at the **organization level**, while usage is still tracked for every individual user.

## Organization-wide allowances

Every active seat contributes usage to your organization's monthly pool.

For example, a company with 10 Revont seats receives:

- **600 Call Hours**
- **1,000 Revont Credits**

A sales rep who needs 80 Call Hours can use them even though each Revont seat contributes 60 hours to the pool, provided the organization has enough total hours remaining.

## Call Hour overages

When your organization exceeds its included monthly Call Hours, calls continue without interruption.

Additional usage is automatically billed based on your plan.

| Plan | Additional Call Usage |
| --- | --: |
| Revont | \$1.00 / hour |
| Revont Pro | \$0.75 / hour |

Admins can monitor total and individual call usage from the Revont Admin dashboard.

## Revont Credit Auto-Recharge

When your organization's Revont Credit balance gets low, Revont can automatically add more credits using the organization's billing method.

There is **no manual credit purchasing**.

Only organization admins can configure Auto-Recharge.

### Auto-Recharge packages

| Recharge | Price |
| --- | --: |
| 100 Credits | \$10 |
| 500 Credits | \$40 |
| 1,500 Credits | \$99 |
| 5,000 Credits | \$249 |

Your organization selects its preferred recharge amount.

For example:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Your organization starts with 1,000 credits">
    A 10-seat Revont organization receives 1,000 included monthly Revont Credits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The balance reaches the Auto-Recharge threshold">
    An admin may configure Auto-Recharge to trigger when fewer than 100 credits remain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Revont automatically adds credits">
    If the organization selected the 500 Credit package, Revont automatically bills \$40 and adds 500 credits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your team keeps working">
    Sales reps continue using Revont without interruption or needing to request additional credits individually.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Admin controls

Organization admins can configure:

- Whether Auto-Recharge is enabled
- The Auto-Recharge credit package
- The balance threshold that triggers a recharge
- Organization usage notifications

Regular users cannot purchase credits or change billing settings.

## Individual usage tracking

Although allowances are pooled, Revont tracks usage by user.

Admins can see:

| User | Call Hours | Revont Credits |
| --- | --: | --: |
| Sarah | 52.4 hrs | 128 |
| Michael | 41.8 hrs | 74 |
| Ryan | 63.2 hrs | 146 |
| Jessica | 34.1 hrs | 92 |

This allows admins to understand usage patterns without having to assign and manage individual credit limits.

## Usage notifications

Revont can notify organization admins as usage approaches configured thresholds.

For example:

- 75% of monthly Call Hours consumed
- 90% of monthly Call Hours consumed
- Revont Credit balance below Auto-Recharge threshold
- Auto-Recharge successfully processed

<Info>
  Auto-Recharge is designed to prevent AI features from becoming unavailable to sales reps because an organization has exhausted its included monthly credits.
</Info>
