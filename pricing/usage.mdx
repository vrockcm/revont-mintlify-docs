# Usage & Auto-Recharge

Revont is designed so sales reps don't lose access to AI tools in the middle of their workday.

Assisted Call Hours, Unassisted Call Hours, and Revont Credits are managed at the **organization level**, while usage is still tracked for every individual user.

## Assisted and unassisted calls

An **Assisted Call** has live Revont suggestions or flashcards enabled. Live suggestions, suggestion warmup, and real-time coaching are covered by the call-hour meter. They do not consume Revont Credits.

An **Unassisted Call** has live suggestions and flashcards disabled. Transcription and other enabled call processing can continue, but no suggestion warmup runs and no live suggestion credits are charged.

Unassisted calls use a provisional **0.67 weighted-hour rate**. This makes the conversion predictable:

| Actual call time | Weighted usage |
| --: | --: |
| 1 Assisted hour | 1.00 weighted hour |
| 1 Unassisted hour | 0.67 weighted hour |
| 3 Assisted hours | 3.00 weighted hours |
| 4.5 Unassisted hours | 3.00 weighted hours |

That means a Revont seat's 3 daily Assisted Call Hours are equivalent to about 4.5 daily Unassisted Call Hours. Revont Pro's 4 daily Assisted Call Hours are equivalent to about 6 daily Unassisted Call Hours. These allowances are pooled across the organization.

The weighting applies to call-hour accounting only. It does not make transcription free, and it does not turn background intelligence into a credit charge.

## Organization-wide allowances

Every paid seat contributes usage to your organization's pooled allowance.

For example, a company with 10 Revont seats receives:

- **30 Assisted Call Hours per day**
- **6,000 Revont Credits per month**

A sales rep can use more than one seat's daily contribution when the organization has enough pooled weighted hours remaining. For example, 10 Revont seats provide 30 Assisted Call Hours or about 45 Unassisted Call Hours per day before the pooled cap is reached.

## Call Hour cap

When your organization reaches its included weighted call-hour allowance, Revont blocks starting a new call until more allowance is available in the next usage period.

The initial rollout does not automatically bill call-hour overages. This keeps call costs predictable while the pooled meter is validated. Any future Stripe metered-call product will be announced and documented before it is enabled.

| Plan | Initial behavior after pooled allowance |
| --- | --: |
| Revont | New calls are blocked |
| Revont Pro | New calls are blocked |

Admins can monitor total and individual call usage from the Revont Admin dashboard.

## Revont Credit Auto-Recharge

When your organization's Revont Credit balance gets low, Revont can automatically add more credits using the organization's billing method.

There is **no manual credit purchasing**. Auto-Recharge is required for active organizations so a team does not lose access because a pooled balance reached zero.

Only organization admins can configure Auto-Recharge.

### Auto-Recharge packages

| Recharge | Price |
| --- | --: |
| 600 Credits | \$50 |
| 1,200 Credits | \$95 |

Your organization selects its preferred recharge amount.

For example:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Your organization starts with 6,000 credits">
    A 10-seat Revont organization receives 6,000 included monthly Revont Credits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The balance reaches the Auto-Recharge threshold">
    An admin may configure Auto-Recharge to trigger when the organization's configured threshold is reached.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Revont automatically adds credits">
    If the organization selected the 600 Credit package, Revont automatically bills \$50 and adds 600 credits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your team keeps working">
    Sales reps continue using Revont without interruption or needing to request additional credits individually.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the automatic payment fails, Revont records the failure and does not grant unbilled credits. Credit-consuming AI actions stop when the pooled balance reaches zero until an organization admin resolves the payment method in Stripe Billing Portal. Auto-Recharge remains required for active organizations.

## Admin controls

Organization admins can configure:

- The Auto-Recharge credit package
- The balance threshold that triggers a recharge
- Organization usage notifications

Regular users cannot purchase credits or change billing settings.

## Organization access and billing roles

Only organization admins can purchase seats, manage the billing interval, open the Stripe Billing Portal, or configure Auto-Recharge. Regular members use the organization's pooled allowances and cannot start a subscription or change billing settings.

If an administrator deactivates a member, that member sees an **Organization Access Is Inactive** screen instead of a subscription or payment screen. The member should contact an organization administrator to request reactivation. Existing organization history is preserved.

## Individual usage tracking

Although allowances are pooled, Revont tracks usage by user.

Admins can see:

| User | Assisted / Unassisted Hours | Revont Credits |
| --- | --: | --: |
| Sarah | 52.4 hrs | 128 |
| Michael | 41.8 hrs | 74 |
| Ryan | 63.2 hrs | 146 |
| Jessica | 34.1 hrs | 92 |

This allows admins to understand usage patterns without assigning individual credit limits. The organization balance remains authoritative because credits are pooled.

## Usage notifications

Revont can notify organization admins as usage approaches configured thresholds.

For example:

- 75% of the pooled weighted call-hour allowance consumed
- 90% of the pooled weighted call-hour allowance consumed
- Revont Credit balance below Auto-Recharge threshold
- Auto-Recharge successfully processed

<Info>
  Auto-Recharge is designed to prevent AI features from becoming unavailable to sales reps because an organization has exhausted its included monthly credits.
</Info>
