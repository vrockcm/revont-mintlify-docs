# What is a Revont Credit?

Revont Credits are the usage currency for user-requested AI actions outside of the live-call meter.

Instead of showing your team technical measurements such as model tokens, Revont converts AI usage into a simple credit system.

<Info>
  Live call transcription, real-time guidance, and call analytics are covered by your **Call Hours** and do not consume Revont Credits.
</Info>

## What uses Revont Credits?

Credits are primarily used when a user actively asks Revont to generate, analyze, or process information.

| Revont action | Credit usage |
| --- | --: |
| Ask Revont AI a question | 1 credit |
| Draft an email | 1 credit |
| Rewrite or improve an email | 1 credit |
| Generate a follow-up | 1 credit |
| Ask a question about your documents | 1 credit |
| Summarize a document | 2 credits |
| Perform an explicit deep document or thread review | 5 credits |

<Note>
  Revont uses fixed, published charges for customer-requested actions. Revont does not expose a vague size-based or “large-context” credit bucket. Background processing is included unless a future plan change explicitly says otherwise.
</Note>

## What doesn't use Revont Credits?

The following are included or covered by your call-hour allowance and do not consume Revont Credits:

- Live transcription
- Real-time AI sales guidance
- Objection detection
- Live call suggestions
- Per-call analytics
- Post-call processing
- Standard call summaries
- Talk-time and conversation analytics
- Suggestion Warmup during an Assisted Call
- Post-Meeting Intelligence
- Meeting Memory and Action Item Extraction
- Email Thread Memory Extraction
- Background embedding and indexing

This separates Revont into two simple usage measurements:

## Credits are pooled across your organization

Each paid seat contributes credits to the organization's shared monthly allowance.

### Revont

**600 Credits per seat**

10 seats = **6,000 shared monthly credits**

### Revont Pro

**1,200 Credits per seat**

10 seats = **12,000 shared monthly credits**

A user can consume more than their per-seat contribution as long as the organization still has credits available.

## Monthly vs. recharged credits

Your subscription includes a new credit allowance every billing cycle.

Included credits reset when your next billing cycle begins.

Credits added through Auto-Recharge are tracked separately and remain available until they are consumed while your organization maintains an active Revont subscription.

Revont consumes your **included monthly credits first**, followed by any remaining Auto-Recharge credits.

Included monthly credits expire at the end of the usage period. Auto-Recharge credits do not silently create a grace balance after a subscription is canceled. If an automatic recharge fails and the pooled balance reaches zero, credit-consuming AI actions are blocked until the organization has a valid payment method and the next recharge succeeds.
