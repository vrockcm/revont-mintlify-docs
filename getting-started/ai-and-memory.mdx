Revont's AI surfaces are distributed across the workflow rather than hidden in one page.

## Where to look

- **Live meeting assistance** is available from the meeting experience when the relevant capability is enabled.
- **Search and chat** are available from the protected app shell and help you find or ask about workspace information.
- **Knowledge Base** is an admin-gated area for articles, folders, imports, and knowledge organization.
- **Meeting detail** can expose memory and evidence connected to the meeting.
- **Coaching** turns analyzed meeting activity into rep-facing and admin-facing insights.

## A safe mental model

Use the AI surfaces to find context and propose next steps. Confirm record links, action ownership, and important customer details before treating generated content as final.

<Note>
  AI output, knowledge access, and meeting visibility follow the features and permissions enabled for your workspace. The page you see can be narrower than the full product surface described here.
</Note>
