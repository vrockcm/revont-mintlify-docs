Communications is the shared workspace for meeting activity and email activity.

The page has two permission-aware tabs:

- **Meetings & Calendar** shows the meeting queue and calendar-oriented activity.
- **Emails** uses a master-detail layout with the thread list on one side and the selected thread on the other.

The header provides the shared search and client/deal filters. The available sharing filter depends on your access to other users' activity.

## Meeting actions

Meeting cards can expose actions such as:

- **Join** when a meeting link is available.
- **Prep** for an upcoming meeting when preparation is available.
- **Schedule Meeting** from the meeting surface.
- **Start Meeting** for a new meeting or a voice memo flow.
- Open the meeting detail page for the full record.

## Email actions

The Emails tab is organized around selecting a thread, reviewing its detail, and using the available compose, share, link, or attachment controls. The exact actions depend on the selected thread and your permissions.

## Sync

Communications exposes a manual sync control. Calendar and email data also use incremental sync paths when their tab becomes active. Sync status and errors are shown in the page header or related dialog.

<Info>
  If only one tab appears, your account may have access to only one of the two Communications capabilities. The app automatically falls back to an available tab when the requested tab is not readable.
</Info>
