**Communications Hub** is the shared workspace for meeting activity and email activity.

Open **Communications Hub** from the sidebar. The header then gives you two tabs:

- **Meetings** opens the meeting queue and calendar-oriented activity.
- **Emails** opens the email workspace.

The sidebar child item is called **Meetings & Calendar**. After you open the hub, the page header uses the shorter **Meetings** label.

## Meeting Actions

Meeting cards can expose actions such as:

- **Join** when a meeting link is available.
- **Prep** for an upcoming meeting when preparation is available.
- **Schedule Meeting** from the meeting surface.
- **Start Meeting** for a new meeting or a voice memo flow.
- Open the meeting detail page for the full record.

## Email Actions

The **Emails** tab uses a master-detail layout with the thread list on one side and the selected thread on the other. Select a thread to review its messages and available compose, share, link, or attachment controls.

## Search, Filters, And Sync

The shared header includes **Search...**, client and deal filters, and a sharing filter when your account can browse shared activity. The header also displays sync status and provides the available sync control.

<Info>
  If only one tab appears, your account may have access to only one of the two Communications capabilities. Revont automatically opens an available tab when the requested tab is not readable.
</Info>
