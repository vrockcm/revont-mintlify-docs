This quickstart is intentionally small. It gets you oriented without assuming a provider, permission, or feature that may not be enabled for your workspace.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in and open Home">
    Launch the Revont desktop app and sign in. The protected workspace opens at Home after authentication.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Learn the sidebar">
    Use the sidebar to open Communications, Actions, Clients, Deals, Contacts, Analytics & Reports, Coaching, and the administration surfaces available to your role. If the sidebar is collapsed, use the expand control or the More menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the services your workspace uses">
    Open Settings from the sidebar. The Integrations tab contains the connected-account controls for Google, Microsoft, and Zoom. Connect only the providers your workflow requires.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Communications">
    In Communications, switch between Meetings & Calendar and Emails. Use the shared search and record filters to narrow the activity shown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a record">
    From a meeting, email, client, deal, or contact, follow the linked record into its detail page. This is where activity and related context become useful for the next step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review follow-through">
    Open Actions to inspect extracted or manually created follow-up work. Use Analytics & Reports or Coaching when you need a broader view of performance.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The exact sidebar and controls are role- and feature-aware. If a page is missing, check with the workspace admin rather than following a direction that assumes access.
</Note>
